How to play

  • ← ↑ ↓ → or WASD to move. Swipe on touch screens.
  • P pauses, M mutes, Enter starts.
  • Gather every seed to clear the level. The side tunnels wrap around.
  • Touch a Shade and your light flickers out. Big Moon Drops make them vulnerable for a few seconds.

Meet the Shades

  • Murk tracks you head-on.
  • Dusk cuts you off, aiming ahead of you.
  • Gloam follows your footsteps.
  • Vesper is erratic. Don't trust a straight line.

Scoring

  • Seed: 10 points
  • Moon Drop: 50 points
  • Banished Shades: 200, 400, 800, then 1600 in one drop
  • Firefly jar bonus appears twice per level. A light is added at 10,000.
