A small meditation on noise

Meaning is often something we search for: a pattern, a message, a destination. But not everything has to mean something.

Here, every key is invited to speak. Letters, numbers and symbols tumble together without hierarchy, without a plan, and without the obligation to become a sentence.

Perhaps this is not chaos, but possibility before it has chosen a shape. You can stop it, let it flow again, or send a little piece of the beautiful nonsense into someone else's day.
